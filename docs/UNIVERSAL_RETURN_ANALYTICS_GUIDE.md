# Universal Return Analytics Guide

## Supported asset classes

- Booster Product
- Secret Lair

## Readiness tiers

- Analytics Ready: at least six observed months spanning four months.
- Established: at least 13 observed months spanning 12 months.
- Advanced: at least 25 observed months spanning 24 months.

## Calculation rules

CAGR uses the first and latest observed monthly prices. Volatility annualizes
observed monthly percentage returns by square root of 12. Missing months are not
filled. Rolling metrics require observed endpoints. Rankings exclude products
that do not meet Analytics Ready status.
