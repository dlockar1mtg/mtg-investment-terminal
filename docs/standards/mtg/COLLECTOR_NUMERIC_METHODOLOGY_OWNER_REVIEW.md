# Collector Numeric Methodology Owner Review

## Purpose

This package converts the eleven unresolved numeric requirements into seven owner decisions. It does not activate methodology, authorize projections, or authorize purchase recommendations.

## Governing rule

Only the canonical MTG standards and explicit owner approvals may authorize production methodology. Legacy equations remain reference material unless separately approved.

## Proposed review sequence

1. Direct-history calibrated method.
2. Direct-history limited method.
3. Comparable-product adjusted method.
4. Fundamental-comparable hybrid method.
5. Scenario, confidence, and uncertainty framework.
6. Supply, demand, liquidity, and reprint adjustments.
7. Caps, freshness thresholds, and purchase analysis.

## Recommended package

The recommendations favor interpretable and backtestable methods tied directly to governed evidence:

- Robust historical return blending for calibrated history.
- A minority direct-history contribution for limited-history products.
- Similarity- and quality-weighted peer contributions for comparable forecasts.
- Comparable-majority weighting for the approved Japanese FINAL FANTASY hybrid.
- Evidence-derived scenario widths, confidence, and uncertainty.
- Bounded and disclosed market-factor adjustments.
- Data-derived threshold proposals followed by separate owner approval.

## What approval would and would not mean

Approval of a decision authorizes implementation of that design for testing and semantic review. It does not automatically authorize production projections or purchase recommendations. Those remain separate certification gates.

## Required approval artifact

A subsequent versioned approval file must record, for every decision group:

- selected option;
- any owner modifications;
- approval date;
- approved implementation scope;
- whether backtesting is required before activation;
- whether the decision authorizes forecast construction only or also purchase analysis.

## No silent defaults

A missing owner selection must remain inactive. Recommended options are proposals, not defaults.
