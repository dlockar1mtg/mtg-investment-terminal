# Phase 10.7.4 — Collector Booster Box Market-Value Admission

This milestone converts the governed Collector Booster Box admission profile into a certified market-value admission ledger.

Policy:

- `FULL_MODEL`: five or more retained governed observations; eligible for model, forecast, and recommendation layers.
- `PROVISIONAL_MODEL`: one to four retained observations; eligible for provisional forecasting but not recommendation promotion.
- `STRUCTURAL_ONLY`: no accepted observations; retained in the governed registry without an observed market value.

Expected certified distribution:

- 36 `FULL_MODEL`
- 11 `PROVISIONAL_MODEL`
- 2 `STRUCTURAL_ONLY`

Generated validation artifacts remain local under `data/validation/phase_10/collector_booster_boxes/`.
