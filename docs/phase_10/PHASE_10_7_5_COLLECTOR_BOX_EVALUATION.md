# Phase 10.7.5 — Collector Booster Box Full Evaluation

This milestone converts the certified 49-row admission ledger into:

- a full evaluation table,
- guarded 1-, 3-, and 5-year scenario bands,
- guarded recommendations,
- a manifest and certification.

The scenario bands are policy-controlled ranges, not historical-return forecasts. FULL_MODEL and PROVISIONAL_MODEL products receive numeric scenario bands. STRUCTURAL_ONLY products remain numerically suppressed. Only FULL_MODEL products are recommendation-eligible, and directional trading actions remain withheld until historical-return and liquidity features are separately certified.
