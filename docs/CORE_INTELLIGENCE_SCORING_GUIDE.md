# Core Intelligence Scoring Guide

All component scores use a 0-100 scale.

## Risk

Higher is worse.

- Volatility risk: 22%
- Liquidity risk: 18%
- Drawdown risk: 22%
- Data risk: 16%
- Forecast risk: 22%

## Confidence

Higher is better.

- Historical depth: 25%
- Observation coverage: 15%
- Forecast confidence: 30%
- Market confidence: 15%
- Model coverage: 15%

## Recommendation score

Higher is better.

- Expected return: 30%
- Forecast conviction: 25%
- Regime support: 15%
- Risk quality: 20%
- Confidence: 10%

Recommendation thresholds are intentionally conservative and are further
restricted by minimum confidence and coverage requirements.
