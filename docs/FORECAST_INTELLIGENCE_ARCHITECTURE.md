# Forecast Intelligence Architecture

Terminal 2.5.5 combines investment scores, historical features, market
intelligence, trend persistence, volatility, and data confidence.

The engine publishes:

- product forecast summaries
- 6-, 12-, 36-, and 60-month forecast ranges
- downside and uncertainty risk
- product market regimes
- conviction and risk-adjusted rankings
- an executive market forecast summary

Forecasts are deterministic model estimates rather than guarantees. The
confidence and uncertainty fields are intended to keep weak-history products
from appearing more precise than the underlying evidence supports.
