# Pre-Collector 83-Product Production Lane Amendment

## Status

Owner-approved amendment to the Pre-Collector-to-Collector Production Parity Plan.

This amendment supersedes every statement in `PRECOLLECTOR_TO_COLLECTOR_PRODUCTION_PARITY_PLAN.md` that describes a 94-product analytical/reference lane or an 11-product reference-only lane.

## Governing owner decision

The active Pre-Collector production lane contains exactly 83 selected products.

The 11 previously described reference-only products are excluded from the active Pre-Collector lane. They must not be carried into:

- product taxonomy;
- forecast-method routing;
- comparable target selection;
- comparable donor selection;
- forecast generation;
- diagnostics;
- rankings;
- purchase analysis;
- purchase recommendations;
- MTG production export;
- UIP delivery.

Historical source records for excluded products may remain archived only for lineage, reproducibility, and auditability. Archived evidence does not grant analytical or comparable authority.

## Selected-lane controls

- Selected production universe: 83 products.
- Excluded production products: 11.
- Reference-only production lane: none.
- Specialty products remain selected and require appropriate family separation.
- Product price may affect forecast economics, affordability, liquidity, and ranking, but does not automatically exclude a selected product.
- Missing or limited evidence must use a governed reduced-confidence or comparable-product route.
- Comparable selection requires certification and owner-approved controls.

## Revised parity scope

Collector parity for Pre-Collectors applies only to the 83 selected products. Every required production artifact must reconcile to the governed 83-product lane, including:

1. canonical selected-product registry;
2. current-price reconciliation;
3. historical certification;
4. normalized supply, liquidity, and comparable evidence;
5. forecast-method routing;
6. approved comparable selection;
7. method-aware forecasts;
8. economic and semantic diagnostics;
9. purchase analysis;
10. MTG export and UIP reconciliation;
11. owner production acceptance.

The broader discovery and source-history records may retain excluded identities for auditability, but active production code must not treat them as part of the governed lane.

## Revised next stage

`PRECOLLECTOR_83_PRODUCT_COMPARABLE_TAXONOMY_AND_METHOD_ROUTING`

Required outputs must contain exactly the 83 selected products and must fail closed if any excluded product enters the active route or comparable datasets.

## Authority

This amendment does not authorize forecasts, rankings, purchase recommendations, automatic execution, or UIP delivery. Each remains blocked until its respective certification gate and explicit owner approval.
