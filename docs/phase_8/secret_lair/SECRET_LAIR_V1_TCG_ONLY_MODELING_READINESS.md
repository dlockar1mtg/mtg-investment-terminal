## Dynamic universe

The Secret Lair universe is rule-defined and refreshable.

The current product count is certification-snapshot evidence only.

Production logic may not require a permanent Secret Lair population count.

New Secret Lair commercial SKUs are expected to enter the system over time.

New products must flow through:

discovery -> identity reconciliation -> governed admission -> TCG evidence -> historical/metadata evidence -> method assignment -> forecast -> uncertainty -> ranking

A new product is not excluded merely because it was released after the current certification snapshot.

## New-product modeling

There is no arbitrary age cutoff.

Products with sufficient governed history may use the fully validated established-product modeling path.

Products with shorter history must receive a separately governed method assignment, potentially including wider uncertainty or fallback/scenario treatment.

Identity-unresolved products remain outside production forecasting until resolved.
