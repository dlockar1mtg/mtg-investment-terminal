## Entry-price analysis

The following break-even entry prices are calculated for each product:

- Q10
- Q25
- Q50
- Q75
- Q90

A break-even entry price means:

the purchase price at which the existing governed terminal-value quantile
would imply a 0% total return.

The model is not re-estimated at a hypothetical purchase price.

These break-even levels are economic diagnostics, not recommendation
thresholds.
