## Comparable definitions

Exact structural comparable:

finish + product family + sealed configuration.

Broad comparable:

another Secret Lair product with at least one governed realized one-year return event.

The target product is excluded from its own comparable population.

No arbitrary top-N comparable selection is used.

No arbitrary minimum comparable count is imposed.

All eligible comparable IDs are persisted.
