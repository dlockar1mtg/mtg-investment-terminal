# Secret Lair Intelligence Calibration Guide

Evidence tiers:

- Historical: at least six observations spanning 120 days; eligible for all recommendations.
- Emerging History: multiple observations or sources; provisional Hold/Watch only.
- Current Price Only: one fresh price; cross-sectional Hold/Watch only.
- Insufficient: no valid current price; remains Insufficient Data.

The calibration does not infer historical returns from a current price.
