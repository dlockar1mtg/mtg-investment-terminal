# MTG price ledger (seed history)

`universal_mtg_daily_consolidated_ledger.csv` is the consolidated daily MTG price ledger (TCGCSV archive and eBay observations, February 2024 to July 2026) for Secret Lairs, Collector and Pre-Collector booster boxes. It seeds the box models (scripts/build_collector_v2_decisions.py); daily prices collected since October 2026 extend it in data/history/boxes/.
